⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only trophy reported as possibly missable. One meditation at Jason's house is offered only during Chapter 1 — in the morning after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia walks to the balcony and invites you to meditate. Accept it. After Chapter 1 it is gone for good, and it is not yet confirmed whether that one counts toward the trophy. Everything else on this list can be done after the story in free-roam. Jump to its guide ↓

Road to the platinum — “Vice Got a Price!”

Difficulty 4/10 · about 200 hours · 1 playthrough · 0 online trophies · no difficulty settings · cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off. Source: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap.

  1. Step 1 — Play the story (60–80 hours). Finish the 5 chapters and enjoy it. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation (see the warning above). Scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study animals with binoculars as you go — both count toward 100% later. Do optional missions as they appear; you need any 100 for the 100% checklist and there are far more than that.
  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist (about 100 hours). After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is roughly 60% of everything in the game, not true 100%: any 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, all robberies, all shooting ranges, all drone races, Bret's quests. Progress from the story already counts.
  3. Step 3 — Cleanup (about 10 hours). Finish the Strangers & Freaks strands, the side-character questlines, and whatever miscellaneous trophies are left. Five trophies are still unconfirmed by the guide sites — this guide says so honestly on each one instead of guessing.

Sources & how this guide was made

Every trophy's name, description and grade was verified on 19 November 2026 against two independent trophy lists that agree on all 55: Exophase and PSNProfiles. PSN progress targets (for example 250 stunts, 42 shooting-range challenges, 14 drone-race locations, 18 meditation spots, 52 areas) come from PSNProfiles. How-to details come from PowerPyx's GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap, and the missable warning is cross-checked with GTA Zone's trophy list. Each trophy below links its own sources.

All guide text is written in our own words — it paraphrases and synthesises, it does not copy other guides. Where PowerPyx marks a trophy's method as not yet known (5 trophies), this guide says so plainly instead of inventing a method. Trophy data is also stored in this app at data/trophies.json.
